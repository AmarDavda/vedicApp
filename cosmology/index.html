Vedic Cosmology – Explore the Lokas and Cosmic Realms

Discover the fascinating structure of the Vedic cosmos through an interactive exploration of its spiritual and material worlds. Learn about the fourteen principal lokas, from Satyaloka, Tapoloka, Janaloka, Maharloka and the heavenly realms to Bhuloka and the seven lower realms of Patala. Explore spiritual abodes such as Goloka and Vaikuntha, along with the different descriptions of Naraka found in traditional texts. Understand the characteristics, structures, dimensions, directions and positions of these realms, along with their presiding deities, divine entities, celestial beings and other inhabitants. Explore how the different lokas are connected and organised within the traditional cosmic framework, with the ability to customise, add, edit and recolour realms in the interactive cosmology map.
