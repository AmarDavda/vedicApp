Explore the World of Vedic Knowledge

Vedic App is a knowledge platform dedicated to exploring and understanding the vast heritage of Sanatan Dharma and Vedic traditions. Discover the structure of the Vedic cosmos, including its spiritual and material realms, the different lokas, their divine entities, residents, structures and directions. Explore Puranic genealogies and ancient family lineages, from Brahma and the great Rishis to Devas, Surya Vansh, Chandra Vansh, gotras and ancestral connections. Access a growing library of Vedic scriptures, including the four Vedas, Puranas, Sampradaya literature and other traditional books of spiritual and philosophical knowledge. All these resources come together in one place to make the exploration of Vedic heritage easier and more accessible.

  • Vedic Cosmology – Explore the Lokas and Cosmic Realms: Explore the universe according to Vedic and Puranic cosmology through an interactive map of the different lokas and cosmic realms. Learn about the seven higher lokas, earthly realm, seven lower lokas, spiritual abodes such as Goloka and Vaikuntha, and descriptions of Naraka. Discover their structures, dimensions, directions, divine entities, celestial beings, inhabitants and relationships. Understand how the different worlds are organised within the traditional Vedic cosmic structure.
  • Vedic & Puranic Genealogy – Explore Ancient Family Lineages: Explore ancient genealogies and family lineages from Vedic and Puranic traditions through an interactive family tree. Trace the lineage beginning with Brahma, including Prajapatis, great Rishis, Devas, their consorts and descendants. Discover Surya Vansh (Solar Dynasty), Chandra Vansh (Lunar Dynasty), royal lineages, gotras, traditional ancestral connections and other Puranic family relationships. Build, customise and save family trees with individuals, spouses, children and historical notes.
  • Vedic Reading Library – Scriptures, Puranas & Traditional Literature: Explore a collection of traditional Vedic and Sanatan Dharma reading materials in digital PDF format. Read the four Vedas, eighteen Mahapuranas, selected Upapuranas, Bhagavata Purana, cosmological texts, Shad Darshan, Sampradaya scriptures, Shikshapatri, Vachanamrita and other spiritual, philosophical and historical books. Search the library by title and access available documents directly in your browser.
