About the Vedic App

Discover the vast knowledge of Sanatan Dharma through Vedic cosmology, Puranic genealogies and an extensive collection of sacred scriptures. Vedic Cosmology maps the lokas, their divine residents, celestial realms, cosmic structures and directions as nested, colour-coded realms, from the spiritual world of Goloka and Vaikuntha to the earthly Bhu-loka and the lower lokas. Vedic Genealogy lets you trace ancient lineages from Brahma, Rishis and Prajapatis to Surya Vansh, Chandra Vansh, gotras and ancestral family trees and also you can build a family tree with consorts, children and notes, starting from the lineage of Brahma. Other Vedic Reading Materials lists Vedic PDFs, such as the Vedas and Puranas, that you can search and read in one free knowledge platform.
