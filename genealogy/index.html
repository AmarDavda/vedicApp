Vedic & Puranic Genealogy – Explore Ancient Family Lineages

Explore the vast genealogical heritage preserved in Vedic and Puranic traditions through an interactive family tree. Begin with Brahma and trace the generations of Prajapatis, great Rishis, sages, Devas, their consorts, children and descendants. Discover important ancestral lineages such as Surya Vansh (Solar Dynasty) and Chandra Vansh (Lunar Dynasty), along with their kings, descendants and connections to other Puranic families. Explore traditional gotras, Rishi lineages, ancestral relationships, caste and community genealogies, and other historical family connections as documented in traditional sources. Create and organise family trees by adding individuals, spouses, children and notes, making it easier to visualise complex generations and relationships. Customise your genealogy, edit existing entries and export your family tree as a JSON file for future use.
